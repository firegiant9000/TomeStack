# Placeholder Class

This file is an original TomeStack test fixture. Every rule in it is invented for testing the Markdown importer.
It describes a twenty-level class with a level table, so a test can import a whole class from Markdown.

## Core Placeholder Traits

Hit Point Die: D8 per Placeholder Class level
Saving Throw Proficiencies: Wisdom, Charisma
Armor Training: Light armor
Weapon Proficiencies: Simple weapons
Skill Proficiencies: Choose two from Insight, Investigation, Perception

### Fixture Pool

Level 1 Placeholder Feature

**Fixture Pool.** You keep a pool of fixture points equal to your level. You regain all of them when you finish a long rest.

### Fixture Rider

Level 2 Placeholder Feature

Once on each of your turns you may add a fixture rider to one test you make. The rider is worth 1d4.

### Fixture Edge

Level 3 Placeholder Feature

Your invented edge grants a +1 bonus to one kind of test, chosen when you gain this feature.

### Fixture Surge

Level 5 Placeholder Feature

You can spend a fixture point to take one extra invented step on your turn.

### Fixture Ward

Level 6 Placeholder Feature

You can spend a fixture point to shrug off a small invented harm.

### Fixture Anchor

Level 9 Placeholder Feature

While you stand still, nothing in this fixture can move you.

### Fixture Reserve

Level 11 Placeholder Feature

Your fixture pool holds twice as many points as before.

### Fixture Rider

Level 12 Placeholder Feature

Your fixture rider is now worth 1d8.

### Fixture Lull

Level 15 Placeholder Feature

When you finish a short rest you regain half of your fixture points.

### Fixture Beacon

Level 18 Placeholder Feature

You shed an invented light that other test characters can follow.

### Fixture Finale

Level 20 Placeholder Feature

Once per day you may end a test early and count it as passed.
