## Known gaps

TomeStack does not do everything yet. Where it falls short, it shows the rule as text and leaves the step to you. If you hit something not listed here, write it down as a gap note.

## Spells

- Spells that are always prepared because of your subclass (domain, oath, patron or circle spells) are text only. Add them yourself.
- If you have more than one source of spell slots, the pools stay separate. You choose which slot to spend.
- Upcast damage, cantrip scaling, spell components, material costs and concentration are text only.

## Attacks and gear

- The net and the blowgun are text only, because their damage is not a dice roll.
- Ammunition is not counted.
- Weapon mastery effects, most fighting styles, two-weapon fighting and bonus damage from features are text only.
- Exports to other tools list equipped weapons only, not attacks that come from features.

## Rules and targets

- Saving throws, areas and ranges are shown as text. Nothing rolls a target's save for you or places an area.
- Rolls for other creatures, and a view for the person running the game, are not planned.

## Homebrew and notes

- Some homebrew edits need a second step. For example, a roll from another entry can be stored but not yet chosen in Homebrew.
- Gap notes cannot be exported as text yet. The print view can include them.
