## What TomeStack is

TomeStack is a character sheet for tabletop role-playing games that runs on your own computer. Your characters are stored on your machine, and nothing is sent anywhere. It works out the numbers on your sheet and shows where each one comes from.

## Adding a character

From the front page you can start in three ways.

- **Try the sample.** Open a ready-made character to look around. You cannot hurt anything, so click on everything.
- **Make my own.** The builder walks you through species, class, background and your choices. You can save a draft at any point and come back later.
- **Bring one in.** Open a file shared from another TomeStack, a backup, or a PDF sheet you already have. You see a preview and decide what to keep before anything is added.

## Rolling and the status bar

Press a roll button to roll with the right bonus already added. The result and the last few rolls show in the status bar at the bottom of the window. The status bar also says when something was saved, or when something needs your attention.

## Backups

Your characters live in one data folder on this computer. Make a backup now and then, and keep a copy somewhere else, such as a cloud drive or a memory stick. Backups can include your attached PDFs. Restoring a backup brings your library back as it was.
